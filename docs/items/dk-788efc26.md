# the card item shape is re-asserted by four producers with nothing to check it

From docs/BACKLOG.md (P1 — real debt, worth a task), originally B2.

Done. The card shape is now declared once as the `CardItem` JSDoc typedef at the top of
`src/lib/github.js` (commit 31de2a3), and every producer and `Card.jsx` reference it.

`<Card>` consumed a shape (`title`, `blurb`, `links[]`, `year`, `tag`, plus `game`/`status`
for mods and `stack[]` for projects) that was declared nowhere. Four files produced it
independently: `lib/github.js` `toItem()`, `data/mods.js`, `data/projects.js`,
`data/manual.js`. With no TypeScript and no tests, renaming a field on `Card` would silently
have broken the other three.

**Resolution.** The typedef lives on the data boundary in `lib/github.js` rather than beside
`Card.jsx`, per the Codex sign-off (a typedef parked next to the consumer "would merely move
the ambiguity"). It is documentation-weight only: not a runtime validator, not a TS migration.

**The `category` asymmetry is gone.** `category` was required on `toItem()` output and on
`manual.js` entries (both filtered on it in `splitRepos`/`mergeManual`) but absent from
`mods.js`/`projects.js`. It is now required on `CardItem`, and the generated fallbacks
(7a4e059) carry it; every producer sets it (71c7559).

*Raised by: abstraction lens (P2), severity sharpened by Codex (agreed, with the placement
correction).*
